Hackathon Feedback – NIT Trichy

Participating in the NIT Trichy Hackathon was an incredible learning experience. The event was well-organized, and the problem statement encouraged us to think creatively and build practical solutions within a limited time. Working under pressure helped us improve our technical skills, teamwork, time management, and problem-solving abilities.

The interactions with mentors and judges were particularly valuable, as their feedback provided new perspectives and ideas for improving our project. It was also inspiring to meet participants from different colleges and learn about their innovative approaches.

Overall, the hackathon was both challenging and rewarding. It pushed us beyond our comfort zone and gave us hands-on experience in building a working prototype, presenting our ideas, and collaborating effectively as a team. I look forward to participating in more such events in the future.
